A letter for you

Read this slowly.

If nobody has told you lately, let me tell you now: I'm glad you're here.

Not because you have everything together. Not because you've figured life out. Not because you've been strong every day. I'm glad you're here simply because you are.

I know there are days when you look around and wonder why everyone else seems to know where they're going. You watch people move forward while you feel like you're standing in the same place, wondering if something is wrong with you.

There isn't.

You are not late. You are not a failure. You are not running out of time.

Sometimes life becomes quiet before it becomes beautiful again.

Sometimes you have to lose the version of yourself who thought she needed to have everything figured out before you can meet the version of yourself who finally learns how to live.

And I know you've probably been harder on yourself than anyone else ever could be.

You've replayed mistakes. You've remembered embarrassing moments. You've wondered what would have happened if you had chosen differently. You've looked at old versions of yourself and wished you could go back and save them.

But listen to me: you were doing the best you could with the heart, knowledge, and strength you had at the time.

You don't have to punish that version of yourself anymore.

She was trying to get you here.

And look at you. You're still here.

Maybe your life doesn't look the way you imagined. Maybe some dreams changed. Maybe some people left. Maybe there are parts of yourself you haven't found again yet.

But your story is not finished just because this chapter hurts.

There are still mornings you haven't watched arrive. There are still places you've never stood. There are still friendships you haven't made. There are still songs you haven't heard at exactly the right moment.

There are still versions of you that haven't existed yet.

The calmer you. The stronger you. The happier you. The you who finally looks in the mirror and realizes that she made it through something she once thought would destroy her.

You haven't met her yet.

So please... don't leave the story before you meet her.

You don't need to fix everything tonight. You don't need a five-year plan. You don't need to know exactly who you're becoming.

Just take the next step.

Then another.

And when you can't take a step, rest.

Rest until you can breathe again. Then begin again.

One day, you may look back at this version of yourself with tears in your eyes.

Not because you failed.

But because you'll realize how incredibly brave you were for continuing when you couldn't see what was waiting on the other side.

You don't have to become someone else.
You just have to keep becoming you.

Before you leave

Keep going.

If you remember nothing else from this little place, remember this:

You are not finished.

There is still so much of you left to discover.

There will be days when you feel like you're finally becoming the person you've always wanted to be. There will also be days when you feel like you've somehow gone backwards.

Both days belong to your story.

You don't have to be perfect to move forward. You don't have to be fearless. You don't even have to feel ready.

Sometimes courage is simply waking up and deciding to give life another chance.

So take your time. Drink some water. Get some sleep. Look at the sky. Listen to your favorite song. Laugh at something stupid. Let yourself have a good day when one comes.

And when the difficult days return, because sometimes they will, remember that you've survived difficult days before.

You don't need to remember how you survived. You only need to remember that you did.

Somewhere in the future, there is a version of you who is grateful that you didn't give up on becoming her.

She is living through mornings you haven't seen yet. She is laughing about things you haven't experienced. She is standing in places you don't know exist.

And one day, she will look back at you — this exact version of you — and realize how hard you tried.

She will remember the nights you cried. The moments you doubted yourself. The times you thought you weren't enough.

And I think she will be incredibly proud.

Not because you had an easy life.
But because you kept choosing to live it.
